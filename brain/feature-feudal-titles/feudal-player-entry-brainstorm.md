# Feudal player entry brainstorm

Problem (2026-09-29): every door into the system needs a prior stake; a conquered held seat only makes the player an occupant, claims need titles, a vacancy vote gives an outsider a fifth of a voice, and the player is excluded from claims.

Idea: a rung below Baron, matching 15th-century gentry. Rungs: make a name (tournaments, deeds) -> retaining by indenture -> manors as assets attached to villages (rent, recruits, prestige; distraint forces knighthood) -> offices (steward, constable, sheriff, JP, warden) -> knighthood by dubbing or distraint -> a patron's promise of a barony as a war reward. Status: brainstorm only, no decisions.
