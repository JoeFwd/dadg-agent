# Scotland settlement budget

Totals 160: Towns 15, Castles 28, Abbeys 14, Villages 103 (2.4 villages per fortification so each fort has 2-3 bound villages). Crown 100 (12/16/9/63), Lordship 60 (3/12/5/40).

Decisions: Inverness to Crown; Dingwall to Lordship; Campbell Argyll holdings stay Crown (wedge against the Lordship); Berwick and Roxburgh become contested ruined villages for a 1471 start. Villages bind to forts by `bound="Settlement.<id>"`. Towns slightly rich on purpose (west needs market anchors). Militia strength is derived, not a settlements.xml field.

Later work deployed the Lordship (see [[scotland-population-project]]).
