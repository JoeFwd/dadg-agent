# Character creation rosters

The age-menu flow dereferences `Horse` and `HorseHarness` of the selected equipment roster. All active DADG role battle sets have both. There must also be a single `player_char_creation_default` roster for Empire. Related intermittent vanilla crash: [[banner-editor-shield-texture-nre]].
