# Feudal system audit findings

The audit separated engine-visible war from abstract war and concluded the war must be map-represented, which led to [[private-wars-stay-in-kingdom-substrate]]. Its critical findings (C1-C3) and others concern the title/occupant split, petition/tension mechanics that were later deleted, and clan-level versus hero-level granularity. Read the source document for the original numbering; many items are superseded by [[feudal-title-transfer-decision]] and [[feudal-hero-holders-decision]].
