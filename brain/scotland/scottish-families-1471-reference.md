# Scottish families 1471 reference

Scope: the 35 `dadg_sco_lord_clan_*` owners in the Map module plus spouses and children already born by early 1471. Notation: `c.` cited estimate, `~` game-age estimate (1471 minus NPC age, not a historical birth), `?` unknown. Hero XML has no birth or death date field; historical deaths are documentation only and children born after the start are absent.

Notable corrections to common assumptions: Ruthven is a laird, not Lord Ruthven until 1488; Home is not Lord Home until 1473; Dunbar of Westfield is not Earl of Moray; Sinclair is Earl of Caithness, not Orkney; MacLean of Duart is Lachlan Bronneach, Lachlan Og is only the heir; MacDonald of the Isles holds both Lord of the Isles and Earl of Ross. Chiefs with unresolved identity or tenure: MacKinnon, MacPhee, MacNeil of Barra and Gigha, Keppoch, MacAlister. Keith numbering follows the modern single Earl Marischal count.
